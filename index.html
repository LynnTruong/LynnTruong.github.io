About
I write from the inside of the culture.

I'm Lynn. A Vietnamese copywriter and content strategist based in Singapore. I've spent 20+ years working across Vietnamese and Southeast Asian markets, in industries from finance to F&B to digital marketing.

I don't just translate. I transcreate. Finding the Vietnamese way to say something that lands with the same emotional weight as the original. I understand the tones, the generational nuances, the Gen Z slang, and the cultural references that make copy feel native.

Most recently I've been building content frameworks at TDCX, working directly within Meta's brand ecosystem and training AI models to recognize content quality. So I understand both the human and machine side of what makes copy work.
